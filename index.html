ABOUT ME

It’s Chelsea, a full stack developer working predominantly with .NET, with a passion for mobile development. Through Tripwire Tech I ship local-first apps such as Castle Adventure Chess (chess learning for kids), Maths For Kids (maths games for ages 5–8), Curling Diary (curling scorekeeping & statistics), Lawn Bowls Diary, Speed Puzzling Diary, and Revenue Tap for publisher analytics.
I am a mum of one with another on the way. I enjoy sharing my knowledge with others and learning new things.
