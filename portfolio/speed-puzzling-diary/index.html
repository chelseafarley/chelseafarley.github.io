Speed Puzzling Diary: speed puzzling timer, drills & progress tracker

Speed Puzzling Diary is a speed puzzling timer and training tracker made by Tripwire Tech. Plan practices, run timed drills, log rounds, and see what actually makes you faster.

What problem it solves

Speed puzzling improves with deliberate practice, but phone timers and scattered notes do not capture drills, tags, reflections, or trends. Speed Puzzling Diary combines a jigsaw puzzle timer workflow with a full speed puzzling tracker so training is measurable.

Who it is for

Competitive and recreational speed puzzlers who want to:

  • Run timed drills and practice sessions
  • Journal competition rounds and reflections
  • Track training frequency and progress over time

Key features

Practice with purpose

Build sessions from drills like Edge Sprint, Color Hunt, Shape Sorting, and Mini Build — or create your own. Run live practice with timers and log results as you go, including durations and custom tags. Save practice templates and reuse the plans that work.

Journal every round

Capture post-practice reflections and competition rounds with notes, ratings, and puzzle details. Optional diagrams help you sketch layouts and piece strategies so you remember what worked under pressure.

Stay organized

Schedule practices and rounds on the calendar. Keep upcoming sessions visible on Home so you can start a planned practice in one tap.

See your progress

Insights show how often you train, how long you practice, and trends over time — so your next session is based on data, not guesswork.

Train on your wrist

Use the Apple Watch or Wear OS companion to control live practice without picking up your phone. Optionally sync workouts and steps with Apple Health or Health Connect (Pro).

Platforms

Available for iOS and Android (phone and tablet), with optional watch companions for Pro. App Store and Google Play links will be added when listings are live.

Speed Puzzling Diary Pro

Unlock custom drills, the Pro drill library, puzzle diagrams, deeper reflection prompts, unlimited practice templates, full historical insights, practice sharing/import, Health sync, and the watch companion.

Frequently asked questions

What is Speed Puzzling Diary?

Speed Puzzling Diary is a speed puzzling timer and training tracker from Tripwire Tech. It helps puzzlers plan drills, run timed practice, journal rounds, and review progress locally on the device.

Is it only a jigsaw puzzle timer?

Timers are a core part of live practice, but the app is a full training journal — drills, templates, competition rounds, reflections, insights, and optional watch control.

Who is it for?

Competitive and recreational speed puzzlers who want intentional practice — solo, in pairs, or as a team — and clearer feedback before race day.

Where is my data stored?

On your device by default. Tripwire Tech does not host your puzzle diary on Tripwire servers. See the sports journal privacy policy.

Which platforms are supported?

iOS and Android on phone and tablet, with optional Apple Watch and Wear OS companions for Pro.
