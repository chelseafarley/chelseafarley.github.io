Curling Diary: curling scorekeeping, practice & statistics app

Curling Diary is a curling scorekeeping and statistics app made by Tripwire Tech. It is built for curlers who want a proper game tracker and practice journal — not scattered notes in a generic notebook app.

What problem it solves

After games and practices, details disappear: line scores, hammer, what you worked on in drills, and whether trends are improving. Curling Diary keeps that history on your device so you can review results, plan the next session, and spot what is working.

Who it is for

Competitive and committed recreational curlers who want:

  • A dedicated curling game tracker for matches
  • A curling practice journal with drills and timers
  • Simple curling statistics and insights over time

Key features

Plan and run practice

  • Build sessions from a starter drill library
  • Timers and completion alerts at the rink
  • Save reusable practice templates (unlimited with Pro)
  • Optional Apple Watch / Wear OS control with Pro

Journal games and sessions

  • Unlimited general and game journal entries
  • Line scores, hammer, venue, rink, and result
  • Nutrition, hydration, mood, and performance ratings
  • Favourite entries and powerful filters
  • Attach workouts from Apple Health (Pro)

See what is working

  • Insights for the last 14 days on Free
  • Full history, Health steps/workouts, and deeper trends with Pro
  • Win/loss heat map and streaks

Stay organised

  • Schedule games and practices with reminders
  • Calendar view of what is coming up
  • Local-first storage with backup and restore
  • Share and import practice templates with Pro

Platforms

Curling Diary is designed for iOS and Android, including phone and tablet layouts, with optional Apple Watch / Wear OS companions for Pro.

Frequently asked questions

What is Curling Diary?

Curling Diary is a curling scorekeeping, practice, and statistics app from Tripwire Tech. It helps curlers journal games, run drills at the rink, and see performance trends — with data stored on your device.

Who is Curling Diary for?

Competitive and committed recreational curlers who want more than scattered notes — including game tracking, practice planning, and insights.

Does Curling Diary keep my data in the cloud?

No. Journal and practice content is stored locally on your device. Tripwire Tech does not host your diary content on Tripwire servers. See the sports journal privacy policy for details.

Is there a free version and a Pro version?

Yes. Core journaling and practice features are available on Free. Pro unlocks extras such as unlimited practice templates, Health sync, watch companions, deeper insights history, and more — via App Store or Google Play subscriptions when the app is live.

Which platforms does Curling Diary support?

iOS and Android on phone and tablet, with optional Apple Watch and Wear OS companions for Pro users.
