Lawn Bowls Diary: lawn bowls scoring, practice & statistics app

Lawn Bowls Diary is a lawn bowls scoring and statistics app made by Tripwire Tech. It is a training and match journal for the green — built for bowlers who want clearer practice, better game notes, and progress you can see.

What problem it solves

Match cards and practice notes are easy to lose. Without a dedicated lawn bowls score tracker, it is hard to remember ends, ratings, and what you drilled last week. Lawn Bowls Diary keeps games, practices, and insights organised on your device.

Who it is for

Bowlers who want:

  • A lawn bowls scoring app for singles through fours
  • Practice templates and drills for draw, weight, and conversion work
  • Lawn bowls statistics and trends over time

Key features

Practice with purpose

Build practice templates and a drill library for draw, weight, and conversion work. Run live sessions on your phone, and keep going from Apple Watch or Wear OS when your hands are full on the rink.

Games and journal

Log singles, pairs, triples, and fours. Capture end scores, ratings, and reflections. Add optional green diagrams for key heads — bowls, kitty, mat, and line — so important ends stay visual, not just written.

Plan ahead

Schedule practices and games on the calendar, set reminders, and keep venues and greens organised in one place.

Insights that help

See trends in your practice and competition results so you can spot what is improving and what still needs work.

Platforms

Lawn Bowls Diary supports iOS and Android (phone and tablet), with optional Apple Watch / Wear OS companions for Pro.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lawn Bowls Diary?

Lawn Bowls Diary is a lawn bowls scoring and statistics app from Tripwire Tech. It helps bowlers journal matches, run practice sessions, and review progress with local-first storage on your device.

Can I use it as a lawn bowls score tracker for team games?

Yes. You can log singles, pairs, triples, and fours, including end scores, ratings, and reflections. Optional green diagrams help capture key heads visually.

Does Tripwire Tech store my bowls diary in the cloud?

No. Diary content stays on your device unless you export or share a backup yourself. See the sports journal privacy policy.

What platforms are supported?

iOS and Android on phone and tablet, with optional Apple Watch and Wear OS companions for Pro features.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks extras such as custom drills, green diagrams, unlimited practice templates, full historical insights, practice share and import, Health sync, and watch companions — via store subscriptions when available.
