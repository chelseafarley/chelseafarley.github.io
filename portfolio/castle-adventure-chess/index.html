Castle Adventure Chess: learn chess through puzzles & quests for kids

Castle Adventure Chess is a chess learning app for kids ages 5–12, made by Tripwire Tech. It turns chess into a kingdom quest — children learn by playing, not by sitting through long lessons.

What problem it solves

Chess can feel overwhelming at first: six pieces, many moves, and special rules all at once. Castle Adventure Chess teaches chess in small steps — puzzles, castles, and guided games — so kids build confidence before playing full matches with friends and family.

Who it is for

Families looking for:

  • A chess learning app for kids that feels like an adventure
  • Chess puzzles for children that still follow real rules
  • A bridge from first pieces to independent games

Key features

Travel the kingdom

Follow a magical map through 12 castles. Each castle teaches one chess idea — from pawns and rooks to check, checkmate, and castling. Earn stars, collect rewards, and replay any castle anytime.

Chess puzzles that teach

Tap a piece to see where it can move. Puzzles guide children step by step with clear goals: reach a square, capture a piece, block danger, or win in the best number of moves. Mistakes are okay — the app encourages exploration, not failure.

Learner Chess

When kids are ready for a real game, Learner Chess adds friendly help: legal moves highlighted, danger warnings, check reminders, and optional hints. Choose Beginner, Assisted, or Normal mode as confidence grows.

Play chess

Play against the computer at Easy, Medium, or Hard, or pass-and-play with a friend on one device — great for family game time and practice between lessons.

Built for young learners

  • Read-aloud support for instructions (Text-to-Speech)
  • Clear board with square labels and visual clues
  • Positive encouragement and star rewards
  • Works offline for puzzles and gameplay
  • Parent Zone with settings, learning report, and progress reset (behind a simple parent gate)

Frequently asked questions

What is Castle Adventure Chess?

Castle Adventure Chess is a chess learning app from Tripwire Tech for children ages 5–12. Kids learn by playing — tapping pieces, solving puzzles, unlocking castles, and growing into real games with friendly assistance.

Who is it for?

Children ages about 5 to 12 who are new to chess, plus families who want a calmer, adventure-style path from puzzles to full games.

Does it teach real chess rules?

Yes. Puzzles and activities follow genuine chess movement and rules, so each step builds toward practical play on a real chessboard.

What is Learner Chess?

Learner Chess is a guided full-game mode with legal moves highlighted, danger warnings, check reminders, and optional hints. Families can choose Beginner, Assisted, or Normal assistance as confidence grows.

Can kids play against each other or a computer?

Yes. Play against the computer at Easy, Medium, or Hard, or pass-and-play with a friend on one device.

Where can I download it?

Castle Adventure Chess is available on the App Store and Google Play for iPhone, iPad, and Android.
